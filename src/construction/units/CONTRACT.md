# Street units

`StreetUnits(architecture,seed,wear)` takes [Atlas architecture](../../architecture/native-schema.ts) and returns the fixed catalogue, [placements, width mappings, overhangs and closures](../../schema/street-kit.ts), receiving ground coverage and original feature descriptors.

[profiles.json](profiles.json) defines every class and zone. `KitCatalogue` builds 208 pieces without plan data: 95 segments, 30 junction arms and far-kerb returns, 42 centres, 30 props and 11 overlays. Run paint, corner seams, palettes and LED fields are baked. Junction paint uses Atlas crossing polygons and stop stations through one shared stripe overlay. Median segments and arms leave the 3.4 m band open for island units: a 2 m nose and 2 m and 8 m islands per zone. Three plain triangular prisms fit saved ground; pedestrian-only centres use that infill. Overlay pieces hold seven arrow combinations, two drain overlays, one scan quad and one stripe. Inventory and compact kit JSON stay within 216 pieces and 3 MB.

`ProfileCatalogue` reports nearest width mappings. `UnitPlan` partitions grade runs and junctions, with each 18 m arm ending exactly at its run start. A return is the crossing street's far kerb between the neighbouring corner seams. Collinear underpass edges continue through their node. Highway edges remain distinct. `ParkingUnits` fits district bays in rectangular notches, square or with 45 degree end returns, to their saved frontage side, slots and ground bands. Each bay places 6 m slot strips and two 4 m returns, each covering 2 m of bay and 2 m of support. Slot lines use 6 m pitch and rear curbs use 2 m pitch. Independent road cores preserve lane paint; walks fill the remaining kerbs. Four kerb palettes follow owner finishes. A bay with incompatible shape, slots, road, support or surface bands is reported in `degraded` and retains ordinary paving. `ParkingFields` supplies the same receiving geometry to catalogue construction and fit validation. `StationPlacements` fits whole pedestrian pieces around shafts. Each Atlas median gets a nose at either station and whole islands between. Fractional remainders scale plain closures. `StreetUnits` emits transforms and shader values under the [placement contract](../../../CONTRACT.md), with feature placement indices retained separately in the manifest.

`placementFootprint(piece,placement)` applies only scale, rotation and position. `UnitCoverage` admits whole instances only where all saved surface roles and levels match. `SourceInfill` fits the exact uncovered fields with unpainted asphalt, district-hex on luxury carriageway, or concrete, using strips and right triangles. Coverage must be complete, surface interiors disjoint and shafts clear. Positive overlap at 0.000001 m2 precision fails with `E_INVARIANT`. Raised collision triangles above 0.05 m must clear every grade driving lane. Tests check decoded underpass geometry, source paint, rejection of duplicate instances and catalogue budgets.
