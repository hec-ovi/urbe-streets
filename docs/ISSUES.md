# Integration review

Engine consumes manifest 0.3.0, the piece kit and placement table. Integration verifies instancing, texture readiness, complete node and placement transforms, collision admission, suppression of replaced ground and module hardware, and Dressing overlap against stable feature bounds.

The loader registers MeshoptDecoder. Cell addresses belong to placement origins. World UV materials and the saved wear field evaluate after placement; noncolliding marking instances apply their authored scan dimensions. Highways and station interactions retain explicit delegated ownership.

Atlas owns candidate planning and reservations. Streets consumes saved ground boundaries without changing the plan.
